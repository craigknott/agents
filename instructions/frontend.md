Use for UI, browser, frontend, design-to-code, games, canvas, and visual QA.

- Match an existing design system when present. For greenfield applications, lead with the working product view rather
  than a marketing page.
- Use familiar controls and icons, and include the states and navigation required by the real workflow.
- Avoid card-within-card layouts, decorative gradient blobs, and palettes dominated by one hue.
- Use imagery or custom visuals when they carry real content or atmosphere, not as a mandatory decoration.
- Keep typography and layout responsive, with no clipped, overlapping, or overflowing content.
- Verify appearance and primary interactions in a browser at representative desktop and mobile sizes.
